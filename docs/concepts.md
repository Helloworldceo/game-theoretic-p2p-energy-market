# Key Game-Theoretic Concepts Used

## Normal-form game
Players choose strategies simultaneously. Payoffs are given by matrices.

## Nash equilibrium
A strategy profile where no player can improve their payoff by unilateral deviation.

## Double auction
Buyers submit bids, sellers submit offers. A clearing price is found that maximizes matched volume under incentive constraints (simplified mid-point pricing used here).

## Stackelberg game
Sequential move: Leader commits first, Followers best-respond. The Leader optimizes taking the Followers' reaction into account.
