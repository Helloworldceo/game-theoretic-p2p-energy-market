# Experiments & Extensions

## Things to try with the current code

1. Change the mix of strategies (more aggressive agents) and observe average clearing price and total volume.
2. Increase the number of agents and see how price volatility changes.
3. Modify the mid-point pricing rule (e.g. use the bid price or the offer price) and compare efficiency.
4. Plot profit distribution across agents.

## Natural extensions

- Continuous strategy spaces (price and quantity chosen from intervals).
- Repeated games / learning (agents adapt bids based on past clearing prices).
- Multi-agent RL on top of the market (connects to Project 2).
- Network constraints (power flow limits between agents).
- Mechanism design: design the auction rules so that truthful bidding is a dominant strategy.
- Coalition formation (cooperative game theory) for community energy storage.

These directions move from simple illustrative games toward the models used in research on local energy markets and transactive energy.
